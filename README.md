# EarthRoot — Permaculture Design & Edible Landscape Service

Welcome to the **EarthRoot Permaculture Studio** web platform repository. This website is a modern, high-performance, single-page web application built to showcase eco-friendly permaculture master planning, edible garden transformations, food forest architecture, rainwater harvesting, and hands-on community workshops.

---

## 🍃 What This Website Is Designed For

**EarthRoot** is created for homeowners, land stewards, urban gardeners, and eco-conscious communities looking to transform traditional landscapes into self-sustaining, biodiverse, and food-producing ecosystems.

### Core Objectives:
1. **Educate & Inspire**: Introduce ecological design principles (Care for Earth, Care for People, Fair Share) and showcase real-world garden transformations.
2. **Promote Professional Services**: Provide clear information on site analysis, custom food forest creation, rainwater harvesting systems, raised-bed kitchen gardens, and soil restoration.
3. **Engage the Community**: Offer registration for hands-on seasonal workshops, soil-building masterclasses, and urban micro-farming sessions.
4. **Seamless Client Booking**: Enable prospective clients to submit detailed project enquiries via an interactive, accessible contact system.

---

## 📑 Website Structure & Content Overview

The platform features a multi-view single-page application (SPA) architecture with 7 dedicated pages/views:

### 1. 🏡 Home 1 (Master Planning Showcase)
* **Hero Banner**: Dynamic headline, key metrics pill counters (150+ Edible Landscapes, 98% Water Efficiency, 40+ Food Forests), and quick CTA buttons.
* **Core Pillars**: Focus on Regenerative Soil, Water Retention, and Food Security.
* **Process Breakdown**: 3-step design workflow (Observe & Map, Multi-Layer Planting, Closed-Loop Nutrient Cycle).
* **"Designed for People" Showcase**: Highlights living spaces designed for comfort, productivity, and aesthetics.

### 2. 🌱 Home 2 (Ecological Systems Breakdown)
* **Interactive Permaculture Zones**: Detailed guide to Zones 0 through 5 (from Kitchen Gardens to Wild Havens).
* **Garden Transformations**: Before & after case studies showcasing soil regeneration and yield growth.

### 3. ℹ️ About Us
* **Studio Philosophy**: Our commitment to natural patterns, biodiversity, and regenerative agriculture.
* **Expert Team**: Profiles of certified Permaculture Designers, Agronomists, and Microbiologists.
* **Sustainability Metrics**: Environmental impact stats including water saved and organic yield produced.

### 4. 🛠️ Services
* **Site Analysis & Mapping**: Topography, sun pattern, and soil assessment.
* **Food Forest Architecture**: Multi-canopy fruit, nut, and perennial herb guild creation.
* **Rainwater Harvesting & Swales**: Passive water catchment, rain gardens, and greywater design.
* **Urban & Backyard Edible Gardens**: Raised beds, vertical trellising, and pollinator havens.

### 5. 🖼️ Portfolio Gallery
* **Filterable Project Grid**: Filter by All, Residential, Food Forests, Water Systems, and Urban Gardens.
* **High-Res Showcase**: Visual highlights of Canopy Layers, Rain Gardens, Wildlife Harbors, and Medicinal Herb Gardens.

### 6. 🎓 Workshops & Education
* **Hands-on Classes**: Seasonal workshops covering Soil Building 101, Backyard Food Forests, and Rainwater Systems.
* **Registration Details**: Instructor profiles, schedule dates, seat availability, and instant booking CTAs.

### 7. 📬 Contact & Enquiry
* **Interactive Booking Form**: Form fields for project type, land size, location, and vision details.
* **Studio Contact Badges**: Modern SVG contact cards for Studio Address, Email Us, Call Us, and Working Hours.
* **Social Connections**: Instant links to Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, and YouTube.

---

## 🎨 Key Features & Technical Highlights

* **Pure Web Stack**: Built with semantic HTML5, modern vanilla CSS3 (CSS Custom Properties & Flexbox/Grid), and lightweight JavaScript.
* **🌓 Light / Dark Theme Switcher**: Toggle between lush green light mode and sleek dark mode with persistent user preferences (`localStorage`).
* **🌐 Dynamic RTL / LTR Support**: Built-in direction toggle button (`RTL`/`LTR`) supporting right-to-left languages with tailored CSS layout flipping.
* **📱 Fully Responsive**: Custom layout scaling optimized across desktops, laptops, tablets, and small mobile viewports (down to 320px).
* **♿ Accessible & SEO-Optimized**: ARIA landmarks, semantic heading hierarchy, alt tags, and fast load times with zero third-party framework overhead.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. **Clone or Download** this repository to your local computer.
2. Open `index.html` directly in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
3. Alternatively, launch with any local HTTP dev server (e.g. VS Code Live Server or `python -m http.server 8000`).
